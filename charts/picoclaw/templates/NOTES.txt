HTTPRoute is disabled. To expose webhook channels (Feishu, DingTalk, WeCom, LINE)
enable httpRoute in values.yaml:
  httpRoute:
    enabled: true
    parentRefs:
      - name: gateway
        sectionName: http
    hostnames:
      - picoclaw.example.com
    rules:
      - matches:
          - path:
              type: PathPrefix
              value: /
